# Plain Terms

Preserve terms defined by `CONTEXT.md` or an external specification.

Replace every other compressed or derived phrase with the established term, or
name the concrete subject, artifact, and behavior directly. Do not add a
glossary entry merely to legitimize unclear wording.

Preserve public identifiers and historical records unless their migration is
in scope. Verify the edited terms across active code, docs, specs, and tests.
